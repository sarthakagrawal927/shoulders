# Shoulders

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Shoulders helps curious software users and builders understand familiar products by exploring their documented foundations, their creators' contributions, and independent ways to use those foundations. Source: owner PRD, 2026-09-14.

## Users

People curious about the software they use and builders considering foundations for their own work. The acceptance test is understanding one foundation and one product contribution without interpreting an unfamiliar graph.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Six curated explorations: VS Code, Supabase, OBS Studio, Firefox, Blender and Godot. Owner expanded scope to silicon and electricity on 2026-09-14. Each has 3–6 direct foundations, in-place explanations, evidence, and shareable product/foundation URLs. Explore deeper along documented connections, explicitly separating implementation evidence, selected platform examples and general physical foundations. Pixel art illustrates selected deeper subjects. No accounts, database, admin, pricing, crawling, exhaustive package resolution, or automatic publication. Relationship types distinguish built with, derived from, powered by, runs on, compatible with, implemented by, made from and requires power. No implication of payment. Editorial text is a research draft until owner review.

## Brand Commitments

Shoulders — The software behind your software. Editorial, spacious, tactile. Owner selected Architectural cutaway (A) on 2026-09-14.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, responsive vertical mobile sequence, readable body text, reduced motion and progressive enhancement.

## Success

Open a foundation, follow another documented relationship, explore a second product, request a specific addition. Hosting and product-specific analytics activation remain release work; no deployment authorized.
